tend.

Your agents run at machine speed. You tend to what matters.

Tend is a calm command center for Claude Code. It shows you only what needs you, briefs you when you step back in, and keeps everything else quiet, so you can run twenty sessions without living in twenty terminals.

01 The idea

Many agents. One thing worth your attention.

  1. Nine tiles are your fleet.Every square is a session, drawn exactly like the fleet strip in the product. Quiet, neutral, working on its own.
  2. One amber tile is what needs you.At the centre, because that's where your attention belongs. Amber is the only accent in the whole brand, and it always means the same thing.
  3. The full stop is a pause."tend." ends with a breath. Calm is the promise: the tool absorbs the noise so you don't have to.

03 Colour

Quiet neutrals. One signal.

The palette is almost entirely graphite, so that the one warm colour carries meaning. Status colours exist for the product, never for decoration.

Ink#121417Background. The calm surface everything sits on.
Slate#20242APanels and tiles
Mist#F3F4F2Light surfaces
Signal#EEA23CNeeds you. Nothing else.
Working#4EC79BRunning fine. Shown softly.
Review#7BA6FFFinished, ready to look at.
Problem#EF7368Stuck, overlap, high risk.
Idle#2B3038Resting. Out of the way.
The amber rule. Amber appears only where a person's attention is genuinely needed, and at most once in a composition. If everything is amber, nothing is.

06 Voice

Calm, plain, on your side.

Calm

We never shout. No exclamation marks, no urgency we don't mean. When something really needs you, one clear sentence says so.

Plain

Say what happened and what to do. "Tests pass. It wants to run the migration." Not "AI-powered workflow optimisation".

On your side

We protect your attention and your evenings. We count what it cost you, not just what got shipped.
